Part 3

Teach: CAD Core Concepts & Tools

10:00
1. What is CAD?

CAD stands for Computer-Aided Design. It allows engineers to build 3D digital objects before 3D printing them in plastic.

2. Moving Shapes

Drag shapes from the palette. Use the black lifting cone to raise shapes up off the workplane on the Z axis.

3. Align & Group

Select multiple shapes and press L (Align) to center them. Press Ctrl+G (Group) to merge or cut holes!

Core Knowledge Check

Hint: CAD stands for Computer-Aided Design. The Align (L) tool uses black dot handles to center shapes perfectly!

PPT Slide Tutorial Guides

5 Essential Design Stages
STAGE 1 Steps 1 to 6: Tube & Pencil Base Alignment PPT Slides 3 - 8
Center Alignment (L Tool)

1. Place Base Tube & Pencil

  • Drag a Tube shape onto the Workplane from Basic Shapes.
  • Switch category to Everyday Items and drop a premade Pencil.
  • Box-select both shapes and click Align Tool (L).
  • Click center black alignment dots to lock pencil in inner tube core.
STAGE 2 Steps 7 to 11: Rotated Wedge & Height Adjustment PPT Slides 9 - 13
Rotate Wedge 90 Degrees

2. Add Wedge Body & Stretch

  • Drop a Wedge shape onto the workplane.
  • Click the curved rotation arrow and turn the wedge 90 degrees.
  • Align wedge with pencil/tube, dragging white corner boxes to stretch height.
  • Use black arrow cone to raise the wedge slightly off workplane.
STAGE 3 Steps 12 to 15: Grouping Wedge & Sphere Hole PPT Slides 14 - 17
Hole Sphere (Finger Groove)

3. Group Wedge & Create Sphere Hole

  • Hold Shift, select wedge + tube, and click Group (Ctrl+G) to merge as one shape.
  • Drop a Sphere onto workplane and change Inspector tab to Hole (Shaded).
  • Flatten sphere into an oval cutter for natural thumb & finger rests.
STAGE 4 Steps 16 to 25: Multi-Side Grooves & Edge Smoothing PPT Slides 18 - 27
Ergonomic Grooves Cut

4. Position 3 Copies & Group Grooves

  • Use Duplicate (Ctrl+D) to make 3 hole sphere copies.
  • Position one hole sphere on each flat side of the triangular wedge.
  • Select wedge + 3 hole spheres (NOT pencil) and click Group (Ctrl+G) to cut finger grooves!
  • Optional: Use Arch Building Block hole cutters to round off outer corners.
STAGE 5 Steps 26 to 28: Pencil Hole Cut & STL Export PPT Slides 28 - 30
Finished STL Ready!

5. Cut Hole Slot & Export for 3D Printing

  • Click on pencil shape and change Inspector tab from Solid to Hole.
  • Box-select grip + pencil hole and click Group (Ctrl+G) to carve central hex core slot!
  • Click top right menu button: Export $\rightarrow$ .STL file to save for 3D printer software slicing!
