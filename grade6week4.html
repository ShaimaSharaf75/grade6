Main Task: Ergonomic Pencil Grip Design

20 Minutes • 35 Points • I DO, WE DO, YOU DO Modeling Workflow

I DO (Teacher Demo)

1. Teacher Modeling (4 Mins):

Watch how to drag a main grip shape ($25\text{ mm}$ tall) and place an $8\text{ mm}$ Hole Cylinder in the middle.

WE DO (Guided Practice)

2. Guided Practice (6 Mins):

Select both shapes $\rightarrow$ click Align $\rightarrow$ center dots $\rightarrow$ click Group to cut out the pencil shaft.
